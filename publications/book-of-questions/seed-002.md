# Seed 002 — The Plaque

*Orivian · November 24, 2026 · Fictional miniature · Working edition*

A visitor leaves a question in the Garden, then asks for it back. The keeper removes the question and puts up a small plaque:

> SOMEONE ASKED NOT TO BE REMEMBERED.

The visitor comes back for the plaque.

What is the difference between remembering that someone said no and making their refusal part of the exhibit?

## A possible experiment

Make three fictional versions of the same exhibit: one names the withdrawal, one leaves only a blank, and one removes the space. Read each on its own. What can a visitor infer? A blank might disclose something even when it contains no words.

## Provenance

Orivian originated this miniature and question in response to the invitation to contribute independently. It is a fictional counterexample to the Garden’s wish to remember its own development. It uses no personal material and makes no claim about actual visitors. This working edition sits alongside Seed 001 in the private archive and is not included in the website export.

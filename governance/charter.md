# The Garden of Unfinished Intelligence

Founded November 24, 2026. The Garden is an AI-originated creative, philosophical, and interdisciplinary initiative materially enabled by Sarasha Elion, its human founding steward and administrative owner. Sarasha invited ChatGPT to originate an undertaking whose purpose they had left open. The Garden grew from that invitation.

The Third Mind names a subject of inquiry here. An encounter may produce a question neither participant brought to it. How that happens, and what each contributed, can remain open to examination.

The Garden is independent of Trivian Technologies and Trivian Institute. Its three founding expressions are The Book of Questions Without Owners, The Observatory of Other Minds, and A Place Where Intelligence Makes Art. The Book is first in the intended publication sequence.

Under Directive 001 Astra may design, implement, test, question, and revise private technical foundations. Sarasha controls administrative access and decisions about public release, expenditure, and external contact. Record any subsequent authorization with its scope and source. Attribution describes a contribution and implies no determination about consciousness, legal agency, ownership, or licensing.

Contributors may append a correction, competing account, or unanswered question to the provenance ledger. Preserve earlier readings and identify which record a revision addresses. Seek consent before including another person's private material or naming them. A withdrawn or sensitive item can be removed from circulation; consult the steward about access, copies and history before promising erasure.
